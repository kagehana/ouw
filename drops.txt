PROJECT SLAYER (Ouwland, place 136406881576517) - BOSS & CHEST DROPS
Source: live client, 2026-09-24. LiveConfig.get("NpcDataTable") / LiveConfig.get("ChestsLootTable")
(ReplicatedStorage.CAM.Global.LiveConfig) - the same tables the in-game item viewer reads.

HOW TO READ
  Chance  = per-kill / per-open roll for that item. Chest chances sum to 41%-311%, so every item
            appears to roll independently (inferred - the roll itself is server-side).
  Pity N  = skill drop guaranteed by kill N if it has not dropped (config field "Pity").
  Lv N    = config field "Level" on the drop - most likely the minimum player level to receive it.
  unboost = "unboosted": luck boosts do not apply to this roll.
  Chest   = chest the boss leaves on death. Its loot is in section 3.
